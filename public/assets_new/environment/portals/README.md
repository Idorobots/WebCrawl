# WEBCRAWL portals

Eight individual 512 × 512 PNG sprites with genuine alpha transparency, including soft energy edges. Cyan/up and violet/down floor-travel variants each include inactive, activation_01, activation_02, and active states.

Play inactive → activation_01 → activation_02 → active at about 8 fps, then hold active. Reverse the sequence to close. This is an activation sequence, not a seamless idle animation. Frames use the same cell crop and scale to retain registration. Place by the suggested ground anchor (256,448), adjusting to your engine’s ground plane as needed.

Use PNG alpha blending. The JPEG is a preview only. Place the portal in front of your room floor and define its travel trigger separately.

Generated with the built-in image tool.
